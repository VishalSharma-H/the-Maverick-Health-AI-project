## Demonstration checklist
- Open the HTML prototype in a browser.
- Explain the three checks: confidence below 80%, group data below 300 records, and no consent.
- Demonstrate one no-warning case and one high-risk case.
- Explain that doctor review remains necessary and that this is not a real diagnostic system.
