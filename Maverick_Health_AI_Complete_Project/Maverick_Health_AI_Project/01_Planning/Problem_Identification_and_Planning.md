# Planning and Problem Identification

## Problem identified
AI can help analyse medical information and support doctors, but incorrect predictions, biased training data and misuse of patient information can create risks. Patients from groups with limited representation in training data may not receive equally reliable outputs.

## Proposed idea
Maverick Health AI demonstrates a simple safety-review layer around an AI-generated prediction. It checks confidence, representation of the patient's group in the sample training data, and whether consent has been given. Cases with warnings are directed for human review.

## SDG mapping
- **SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being:** safer and more responsible use of AI-supported healthcare.
- **SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure:** applying technology and innovation to healthcare services.
- **SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities:** checking for possible under-representation of patient groups.

## Proposed safeguard
Add bias-checking and human review before important AI-supported healthcare decisions are acted upon.
