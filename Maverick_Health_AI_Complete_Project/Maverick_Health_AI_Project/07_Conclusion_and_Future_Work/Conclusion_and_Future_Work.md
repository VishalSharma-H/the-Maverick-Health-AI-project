# Conclusion and Future Work

## Conclusion
Maverick Health AI is an educational prototype focused on responsible AI use in healthcare. It demonstrates confidence, training-data representation and consent checks, and shows when a human should review a case. It supports the project's main idea of making AI-supported healthcare safer and more inclusive.

## Limitations
- It does not generate medical predictions or diagnoses.
- It uses illustrative training-data counts and fixed thresholds.
- It has no backend, database, authentication or connection to real patient records.
- Its checks are not clinically validated and cannot replace professional judgement.

## Future work
1. Add secure backend and database storage with appropriate access controls.
2. Use ethically sourced, representative and properly governed datasets.
3. Evaluate bias and model performance with qualified healthcare experts.
4. Integrate a validated medical AI model only with clinical oversight and regulatory review.
5. Improve privacy, consent management, audit logs and accessibility.
6. Test the system with a broader range of scenarios and document results.
